Skill: Planning
Skill Description: Planning is the process of creating a sequence of actions to achieve a specific goal. In this problem, the robot must plan the sequence of operations needed to paint both the ladder and the ceiling, ensuring that it follows the constraints imposed by the environment.

Problem: Robot Painting Problem

Problem Description: The Robot Painting Problem involves a robot that needs to paint a ladder and a ceiling. The robot can move between the floor and the ladder. The ladder can be either dry or wet, and the ceiling can be either dry or painted. The primary objective of the Robot Painting Problem is to select a series of legal moves to go from an initial state where the robot is on the floor, the ladder is dry, and the ceiling is dry, to a goal state where both the ladder and the ceiling are painted.

Nouns: robot, ladder, ceiling, floor, state, moves, goal state

Verb phrases: paint ladder, paint ceiling, move to ladder, move off ladder

Stative verbs: on the floor, on the ladder, dry, wet, painted
